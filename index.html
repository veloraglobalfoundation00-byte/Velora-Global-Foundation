WHO WE ARE

About VELORA GLOBAL FOUNDATION

VELORA GLOBAL FOUNDATION is an independent foundation committed to education, youth empowerment, humanitarian support and community development.

We believe that every child deserves an opportunity to learn, develop their abilities and build a better future. We also believe that community development is a shared responsibility involving individuals, organisations, institutions and government.

Our purpose is to bring people together around practical solutions, identify genuine community needs and support initiatives that can make a meaningful difference.

OUR MAIN EDUCATION INITIATIVE

School Children Support Project

This initiative is specifically designed to support 150 school children by providing essential school materials and basic educational needs.

We recognise that some children face difficulties attending school with complete uniforms, school bags, books, writing materials and other necessities. These challenges can affect their confidence, participation and learning experience.

Through this project, VELORA GLOBAL FOUNDATION aims to reduce some of these difficulties and encourage children to remain focused on their education.

OUR CENTRAL PRIORITY IS THE CHILDREN.

This is a school children support initiative. The main purpose is to provide practical educational assistance to children. The launch ceremony is only an opportunity to present the project and distribute the materials. It is not the main objective.

What We Aim to Provide

  • School uniforms produced for the beneficiaries.
  • School bags and exercise books.
  • Pens, pencils and other writing materials.
  • School shoes and socks.
  • Textbooks and reading materials.
  • Mathematical sets and learning materials.
  • Basic food and hygiene support where needed.

PROJECT PRESENTATION

Official Launch and Distribution Ceremony

The foundation intends to organise an official ceremony to present the project and distribute the school materials to the selected children.

The ceremony will bring together invited guests, community representatives, education stakeholders, partners and supporters who share the vision of improving children's access to education.

What the Ceremony Will Achieve

  • Present the school support initiative.
  • Distribute uniforms and educational materials to children.
  • Recognise partners and supporters.
  • Encourage community participation in education.
  • Promote accountability in the use of donations.
The ceremony is part of the project's implementation. The children and their educational needs remain the central priority.
